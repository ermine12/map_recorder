How to use Map Recorder

Map Recorder compares a recorded walk with OpenStreetMap (OSM) and paths you already saved. It proposes parts that may be new. You decide which parts are real and walkable. Keep the original Organic Maps recording as a backup.

Review a recorded walk

  1. Open Delta, choose Load Track, and select the original GPX or GeoJSON file. You can choose Skip GPS when reviewing a file; live GPS is only for the Recorder. Delta sends the walk's map area to OSM lookup servers, but the recording stays on your device.
  2. Start with Road Buffer 4.5 m and Min Length 3 m. Choose Extract. The app needs internet access to compare with OSM.
  3. Read the summary. Open Map layers to see the colors and switch lines on or off. These switches affect the display only.
  4. Open Removed or skipped pieces. Select Show for a blue OSM, purple Master, or tiny skipped section to see where it is and why it was excluded. Compare it with the gray original walk and the base map.
  5. Select Show for each proposed path. The selected piece turns green and the map zooms to it. The floating Map button hides the controls; Controls brings them back without losing your review.
  6. Tick only paths you recognize as real and walkable. Amber Check GPS pieces are near a pause, jump, or tangle and need extra care.
  7. Choose Save Approved to store checked paths in this browser, or Approved GeoJSON to download them for your other map. You can do both. Clear clears this review, not the saved master.

What the colors mean

LayerMeaning
Gray · Original walkThe observed walk, separated at known gaps.
Blue · On OSMParts near mapped roads, excluded from new proposals.
Purple · In MasterParts already close to a saved path.
Orange · New pathA proposal waiting for your approval.
Amber · Check GPSA proposed piece affected by suspicious recording quality.
Dashed gray · Tiny skippedA short piece excluded by Min Length or by the Master's fixed 3 m minimum. Select Show for its reason.
Red markers · Gaps / tanglesEndpoints of a recording gap or uncertain tangle boundary; the app does not claim a path between them.

If a decision looks wrong

A real path close to a mapped road may disappear with a large Road Buffer. Lower the buffer and choose Extract again. A small buffer can leave GPS drift beside a road. Lower Min Length to inspect a short connector skipped after OSM comparison. A tiny piece left after Master comparison still has a fixed 3 m minimum. These settings help you inspect uncertain cases; they cannot prove where a path lies.

Keep doubtful pieces unchecked. Walk the place again or correct the source recording if needed. The app isolates tangles but cannot reconstruct the true route through bad GPS data.

Recorder, Master Map, and backups

In Recorder, choose Start, walk, then Stop. Review for Master sends that recording through the same Delta checks. In Master Map, use Review Past GeoJSON to inspect an older file.

The master lives in this browser's local storage. Use Export Clean Master for a backup. Paths saved before the review checks may still contain old tangles. Export a backup, clear the old master, then process the original recordings again through Delta.

The approved GeoJSON is a map overlay. A navigation app still needs checked junctions and building entrances before it can route along these paths.
